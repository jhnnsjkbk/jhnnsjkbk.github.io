### Hi there, I'm Johannes!

I am a **Research Scientist** within the Modeling & Algorithms for Impact team at IBM Research Europe. I co-lead work at the **intersection of deep learning, quantum computing, and physical systems**.

In the context of **quantum computing**, I work to make quantum optimization algorithms more practical, using deep learning to reduce the classical overhead and hardware bottlenecks that currently limit them. In the context of **physical systems**, I co-lead IBM Research's activities on **deep learning for our planetary system**, pretraining and scaling multimodal deep models in **collaborations with NASA, ESA, and the EU Horizon program**.

My work has been awarded the **NASA Agency Group Award**, the **NASA Marshall Space Flight Center Honor Award**, and several **IBM accomplishment awards**, and has been featured in international and national media. I graduated with **summa cum laude** from KIT and ETH, where my research spanned across all relevant subfields of deep learning-based systems: data-centricity, model-centricity, and human-centricity. My thesis on data-centric AI has been **awarded a national-level dissertation award** by the VDE -- one of the largest technology organizations in Europe. I also received a **best paper award**, and a best paper award nomination for theoretical contributions to human-centric AI. In fall 2024, I was fortunate enough to be recommended as a top candidate for a tenure track professorship at a German university of excellence. Together with a range of amazing co-authors, my work has been **published in highly recognized journals and conferences**.

I am fortunate enough to **co-supervise several world-class Ph.D. students** at ETH Zurich and Trinity College.

On weekends, you will find me somewhere in the mountains with my wife, our son, and our dog! :mountain:
